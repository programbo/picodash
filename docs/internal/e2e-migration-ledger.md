# E2E migration ledger

This is the coverage ledger for the agent-first test cutover. The baseline was produced with
`bun x playwright test --list` from `apps/web` on 2026-07-30: **140 collected tests in nine files**.
The count includes the skipped constrained-hybrid test.

Every baseline title is listed below. Parameterized titles are kept together only when they are one
source test and have one destination. During cutover, update a row if its final owner or title
changes; do not delete an old test until its admitted replacement is green.

## Dispositions and target keys

- **Retain**: keep the browser contract substantially intact in the named browser journey.
- **Merge**: keep its unique assertions inside the named cohesive journey; remove the standalone
  test.
- **Move**: prove the contract at the named lower layer or other browser suite; remove it from the
  current suite.
- **Delete**: the assertion is obsolete, implementation-only, or already covered; no replacement
  browser test is admitted.

## Cutover accounting

The target is intentionally not a one-for-one rewrite:

- Store, UI, DashPanel, and DashList tests absorb their deterministic matrices before browser
  sources are removed.
- The Contract Lab keeps the browser-only assertions in cohesive journeys across its six presets.
  It must collect **40 or fewer tests**, including all annotations and expansions.
- Website journeys remain independent and cover only the public site.
- The old nine-file mixed suite, old Lab routes, query fixtures, and the skipped test are deleted
  once every non-deleted row above has a green target.
- No row may be parked in a legacy, slow, nightly, quarantined, skipped, or hidden suite.
